Recently, out of nowhere, I was looking at my phone's screen time stats and I
realised that I spend shitload of my time on YouTube. I got into tech in 2017.
Since then, it's been my go-to platform for learning new things, staying
updated with tech trends and unwinding with some light-hearted or weird
content.

Over the years, I curated a personal playlist of videos that have either taught
me something valuable or I've just watched them for no reason.

This page is a collection of those gems. I wanted to share these so you don't
have to sift through the algorithm yourself. If even one of these ends up in
your recommended feed and makes your day slightly less shit, I'll call that a
win.

.. note::

    These videos aren't sorted but are categorised based on their content. I
    don't own any of the videos listed here. All credit goes to the respective
    creators. Please support them by subscribing to their channels if you like
    their content.
